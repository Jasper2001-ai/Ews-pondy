Daily station allocation

Full-day absence redistributes all stations once; first-half absence redistributes half; second-half absence uses admin-selected markets.
Allocation rule: split stations into equally sized consecutive blocks among eligible members. Do not use members' original station totals, other responsibilities or historical workload as balancing weights. First-half absence takes half of that member’s master station list (19 of 38). For second-half absence, the admin selects the specific markets. Full-day absence redistributes the full station list once. Overloaded members are excluded only for the affected period.
